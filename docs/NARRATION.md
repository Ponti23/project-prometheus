## Plain text for pasting

```
What if the power went out tomorrow, and stayed out? Meet Prometheus: humanity's backup brain.

Prometheus is a personal AI that already knows your household. This family has two adults, three kids, a dog, and daily medication.

Every answer starts with a real tool call. NVIDIA Nemotron, running on Nebius Token Factory, reads the saved profile instead of guessing.

So, how ready are we for thirty days? The numbers come from a deterministic calculator, not the model. Food is counted in calories, not cans, and it reports coverage of planning targets, never a survival probability. It proposes restock tasks, but nothing changes until I approve.

Approved tasks join the household checklist. When I mark the water done, it goes into the inventory, and coverage recalculates from thirty-three to forty percent.

Now the emergency: the grid is down. Prometheus starts with triage. Is anyone hurt? Can you smell gas or smoke? Then it builds a plan from what this family actually has: water, a wind-up radio, and the medication.

Every source link comes from the tools themselves: the Red Cross, Emergency WA, the CDC. The model can't invent a citation.

I approve the plan, and it becomes a saved checklist the family can tick off as they go.

But surviving isn't enough. Ask how electricity works, and Prometheus opens the Rebuild tree: a learning path from energy basics to small off-grid solar, checked against your inventory, with mains wiring kept professional-only.

It explains the science in plain language, and it remembers. Reload, and everything is still here, in your own database.

Under the hood: the real model Nebius reported, response IDs, latency, tokens, and every tool call. The log proves proposals only change data after you approve them.

Prometheus. Prepare today. Survive tomorrow. Rebuild the future.
```
