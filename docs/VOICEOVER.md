# Voiceover script

Read this over the recorded demo. Each block matches a scene in [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md). About 340 words, which is roughly 2:30 at a calm pace and leaves room for pauses while the model replies. If a scene in your video runs shorter, drop the sentence in *italics*.

**0:00–0:20 · Meet Prometheus**
What if the grid went down tomorrow? Most of us have no plan. This is Prometheus, a personal AI companion running on NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory. It knows my household, and instead of a scary percentage it tells me what's next: eight things left to be ready for three days.

**0:20–1:00 · Prepare**
I just tell it what I did. "I bought five litres of water." Watch the flame: it flies to my water card and pencils in the change. Sixty litres becomes sixty-five, and seven things are left. Nothing is saved until I tap Save. Then it stamps the number and flies home. *And every number has a "Why?": it comes from a calculator built on cited guidelines, not from the model.*

**1:00–1:20 · Auto mode**
Like Claude Code, Prometheus has permission modes. In Auto, small changes save themselves, but only when the number came from me. "A slab of water"? It had to guess, so it asks first. Anything automatic can be undone.

**1:20–1:55 · Survive**
Now the bad day. The power's out across the street. Prometheus switches to Survive, pulls up the blackout guide, and drafts a plan for my family, in pencil, with sources from its tools. I save it and tick off steps as I go.

**1:55–2:15 · SOS**
And if things get serious, there's SOS. No AI, no internet needed. Is anyone hurt? What's happening? One safety question at a time, then one step at a time. *Every word comes from curated guidance.*

**2:15–2:35 · Rebuild**
Survival isn't the end. I ask it to teach me how electricity works. Rebuild opens lesson one of a path toward a small off-grid power system, checked against what I own. *Mains wiring stays theory only. Safety comes first.*

**2:35–2:55 · Technical proof**
Under the hood: the model ID Nebius reported, the response ID, latency, tokens, and every tool call with its arguments. Each change sits pending until I approve it.

Prometheus. Prepare today. Survive tomorrow. Rebuild the future.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room with a phone or headset mic, about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read one scene per take and line them up in your editor. That's easier than one perfect take.
- If your video's scenes are timed differently, shift the blocks to match. Don't speed up your voice.
