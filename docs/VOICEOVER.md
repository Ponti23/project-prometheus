# Voiceover script

Read this over the recorded demo. Each block matches a scene in [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md). About 300 words, which is roughly 2:15 at a calm pace and leaves room for pauses while the model replies. If a scene in your video runs shorter, drop the sentence in *italics*.

**0:00–0:20 · Meet Prometheus**
What if the grid went down tomorrow? Most of us have no plan. This is Prometheus, a personal AI companion running on NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory. It already knows my household: two adults, three kids, a dog, and the medication we can't run out of.

**0:20–1:05 · Prepare**
I ask how ready we are for thirty days. Watch the tool calls: it calculates readiness, finds what's missing, and proposes restock tasks. The numbers don't come from the model. They come from a calculator built on cited guidelines. *That's why it says "coverage of planning targets", never a survival probability.* Nothing changes until I approve it. I approve, mark the water as bought, and the coverage updates straight away.

**1:05–1:45 · Survive**
Now the bad day. The power grid has collapsed. Prometheus starts with safety: carbon monoxide, medical needs, fallen power lines. Then it builds a plan for my family: five people, thirty days of water, the wind-up radio, the medication. Every reply lists its sources, taken from what the tools actually returned. I approve the plan and tick off steps as I go.

**1:45–2:20 · Rebuild**
Survival isn't the end. I ask Prometheus to teach me how electricity works so we can restore power. It opens the Rebuild tree: a learning path from the basics up to a small off-grid DC system, checked against what's in my inventory. I have the solar charger. I don't have a power station. *And mains wiring stays professional only. Safety comes first.*

**2:20–2:35 · Persistence**
I reload the page. The household, inventory, tasks, plan and conversation are all still here. Prometheus remembers.

**2:35–2:50 · Technical proof**
Under the hood: the model ID Nebius reported, the response ID, latency, tokens, and every tool call with its arguments. The log shows each action pending first and changing state only after my approval.

Prometheus. Prepare today. Survive tomorrow. Rebuild the future.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room with a phone or headset mic, about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read one scene per take and line them up in your editor. That's easier than one perfect take.
- If your video's scenes are timed differently, shift the blocks to match. Don't speed up your voice.
