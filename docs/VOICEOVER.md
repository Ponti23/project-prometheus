# Voiceover script

Read this over the recorded demo. Each block matches a scene in [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md). It's written to be spoken: short sentences, contractions, and "..." where you pause. About 2:20 at a relaxed pace, which leaves room for the model's replies.

**0:00–0:20 · Meet Prometheus**

What would you do if the power went out tomorrow... and didn't come back?

Most of us don't have a plan. So I built one.

This is Prometheus. A personal AI companion, running on NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory. And it already knows my household. Two adults, three kids, a dog... and the medication we can't run out of.

**0:20–1:05 · Prepare**

Let's start with the basics. How ready are we for thirty days?

Watch the tool calls. It checks our readiness, finds what's missing, and suggests restock tasks.

Here's the important part. The numbers don't come from the AI. They come from a calculator, built on real, cited guidelines. That's why it talks about coverage of planning targets... never a survival percentage.

And nothing happens without me. I approve the tasks, mark the water as bought, and our coverage updates right away.

**1:05–1:45 · Survive**

Now, the bad day. The grid has gone down.

Prometheus doesn't panic, and it doesn't skip ahead. Safety comes first. Carbon monoxide. Medical needs. Downed power lines.

Then it builds a plan for my family. Five people. Thirty days of water. The wind-up radio. The medication.

Every answer shows where it came from, with real sources pulled straight from the tools. I approve the plan, and tick things off as we go.

**1:45–2:20 · Rebuild**

But surviving isn't the end of the story. Eventually, you want the lights back on.

So I ask Prometheus to teach me how electricity works.

It opens the Rebuild tree. A learning path from the basics, all the way up to a small off-grid system, checked against what we actually own. I've got the solar charger. I don't have a power station.

And anything involving mains wiring? That stays with the professionals.

**2:20–2:35 · Persistence**

Now I'll refresh the page.

Everything's still here. The household, the supplies, the plan, the whole conversation. Prometheus remembers.

**2:35–2:50 · Technical proof**

And for the curious, here's what's under the hood. The exact model Nebius reported, response times, tokens, and every single tool call. Nothing changes until I approve it.

Prometheus. Prepare today. Survive tomorrow. Rebuild the future.

## Recording tips
- Record in a quiet room with a phone or headset mic, about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read one scene per take and line them up in your editor. That's easier than one perfect take.
- Smile on the first and last lines. People can hear it.
- If your video's scenes are timed differently, shift the blocks to match. Don't speed up your voice.
